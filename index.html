CASE PAPER 1 · SUGGESTED TIME ~10 MIN

The Mystery of Mr. Anderson's Condition

You and your medical team have just received an urgent case. Mr. Anderson, a 45-year-old patient, has been diagnosed with thyroid cancer. The doctors at Radiation Research Labs have decided to treat him with a radioactive isotope called Iodine-131. It's your job to understand how this isotope will affect his body over time.

Radioactive isotopes

  • Some elements have unstable isotopes that lose energy over time. This process, known as radioactive decay, releases radiation that can be used to treat diseases.
  • In Mr. Anderson's case, Iodine-131 will target his thyroid and help destroy cancer cells.

What is half-life?

  • Every radioactive isotope decays at a specific rate, measured by its half-life — the time it takes for half of the substance to lose its radioactivity.
  • For Iodine-131, the half-life is 8 days, meaning half of the radioactive material will be gone in 8 days, and half of what's left will disappear after another 8 days.
Iodine-131
Used for: targeting thyroid cancer, since iodine is absorbed by the thyroid gland.
Half-life: 8 days.
